How to play: The way to play Crafty Crafters is simple! Use the dropdown menu to pick an item from the selector. Click Buy to add it to pay as it gets added to your inventory, or Sell to get money back for one you own. If the selected item has a recipe, it'll show above, click Craft to turn its ingredients (from your inventory) into the finished item. The goal is to make money by selling cafted items for a profit.
